# MOONDA: full reference list

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Lunar Landing Site Navigator · Team MOONDA (Phnom Penh).

Every dataset, paper, service, asset and tool used in MOONDA, grouped by source. Each source appears once, with the exact files or pages we used underneath.

MOONDA is not an official NASA product and implies no NASA endorsement. Third-party data, models, fonts and music keep their own licences.
